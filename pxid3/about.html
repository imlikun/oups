Who We Are

PXID is a leading electric mobility ODM manufacturer headquartered in Shenzhen, China. Founded in 2013, we've grown from a design studio into a full-cycle manufacturing partner with 40+ R&D engineers, 200+ international patents, and 200,000+ annual production capacity. We serve 50+ brands across 30+ countries, providing end-to-end solutions from industrial design to mass production.
