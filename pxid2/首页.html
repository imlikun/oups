Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

What is the MOQ for OEM/ODM orders?OEM/ODM订单的最低起订量是多少？+

OEM orders start at 50 units, ODM solutions at 100 units. Joint development projects can negotiate MOQ. Small-batch trial orders are welcome.

OEM贴牌订单最低起订量为50台，ODM研发方案最低100台。联合开发项目可协商MOQ。小批量试单欢迎联系我们。

How long does it take from concept to mass production?从概念到量产需要多长时间？+

Standard ODM projects take 4-6 months: industrial design 1 month, engineering 1.5 months, mold making 1 month, trial production 1.5 months. Using existing molds can shorten to 2-3 months. Prototypes delivered in 15 days.

标准ODM项目从概念到量产通常需要4-6个月：工业设计1个月，工程开发1.5个月，模具制作1个月，试产验证1.5个月。使用现有公模可缩短至2-3个月。15天交付原型。

Can you help with certification for my target market?能帮我做目标市场的认证吗？+

Yes. Products already have CE/UL/RoHS. For specific country certifications (e.g., US CPSC, Japan PSE), we assist with testing and share costs based on order volume.

是的。产品已具备CE/UL/RoHS等基础认证，如需特定国家认证，我们可协助安排测试并根据订单量分担费用。

Do you offer after-sales support and spare parts?你们提供售后和配件支持吗？+

Yes: 3-year warranty on core components, 48h spare parts dispatch, remote tech support, repair manuals and training videos. For large clients, we offer spare parts inventory planning and on-site support.

提供核心部件3年质保、48小时内配件发货、远程技术支持、维修手册和培训视频。大客户可提供备件库存建议和现场支持。

Can I visit your factory?可以参观工厂吗？+

Absolutely! We are located in Shenzhen. Please schedule in advance for factory tour, product demo, and technical discussion. Virtual tours also available.

非常欢迎！我们位于深圳，请提前预约工厂参观、产品展示和技术交流。也可进行虚拟工厂参观。

Can you help with product registration and homologation in my country?你们能协助做产品准入注册吗？+

Yes. We have experience with product registration and type approval in EU (e.g., CoC, WMI), US (DOT, EPA), Japan (PSE, type approval), and other markets. We guide you through documentation and coordinate with local testing agencies to get your products road-legal.

是的。我们在欧盟（如 CoC、WMI）、美国（DOT、EPA）、日本（PSE、型式认证）等市场的产品注册和准入方面有丰富经验。我们协助准备文件并协调当地检测机构，确保产品合法上路。
